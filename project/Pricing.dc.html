The fine print

Membership and Purchase Details

Recurring Monthly Billing

Monthly memberships automatically renew each month on the original billing date until canceled by the member. Membership, class pack, and drop-in payments are non-refundable once processed. It is the member’s responsibility to maintain a valid payment method on file. If a membership payment fails, SUNSWEAT may restrict class booking and membership access until the outstanding balance is resolved.

No Long-Term Commitment

All SUNSWEAT memberships are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. Memberships may be canceled anytime through the SUNSWEAT app. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period, and no partial-month refunds are provided.

Founding Memberships

Founding rates remain locked while the membership remains active. If a Founding membership is canceled, the founding rate is forfeited and cannot be reinstated.

Membership Pause

Members may pause their membership for temporary breaks. Pause requests must be submitted in advance and cannot be applied retroactively. During an approved pause, membership billing and class access will be suspended for the approved pause period. Membership billing and access will automatically resume when the pause period ends. To request a pause, members must contact SUNSWEAT directly.

Account & Class Credit Policy

Each client must maintain their own individual SUNSWEAT account. Memberships, class packs, drop-ins, class credits, and other purchased services are intended solely for the account holder and may not be shared, transferred, or resold. Clients may not book classes under another person’s name or use another client’s membership or class credits. Unused or expired class credits will not be refunded, transferred, extended, or converted to cash or account credit.

Guest Passes

Guest passes are intended for the member’s invited guest and may not be transferred, sold, or exchanged for cash or account credit. Guests must create their own SUNSWEAT account and complete all required registration and waiver requirements before attending class.

Retail and Additional Purchases

All retail, merchandise, and in-studio purchases are final sale.
